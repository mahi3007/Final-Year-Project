1. Executive Summary & Epistemic Governance Boundary

Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) deployed in real-world streaming environments encounters continuous distribution shift caused by diverse speakers, non-native accents, acoustic reverberation, and background noise. Continual Test-Time Adaptation (CTTA) algorithms (e.g., SUTA, CoTTA) update model parameters online from incoming unlabelled audio streams via entropy minimization and teacher-student consistency. While prior literature uniformly reports average Word Error Rate (WER) improvements, this research uncovers a critical, overlooked failure mode: adaptation-induced subgroup regression and disparity amplification.

⚠ The Epistemic Boundary Principle (Issue 1 Governance Invariant)
This project strictly separates four fundamental dimensions of scientific integrity:
  1. Protocol Design: Formal mathematical specifications, estimands, and preregistered hypothesis boundaries.
  2. Implementation Verification: Automated software tests (90/90 passing) proving data isolation, reproducibility, and contract conformance.
  3. Experimental Evidence: Empirical benchmark measurements gathered under strict air-gapped streaming protocols.
  4. Scientific Conclusion: What the empirical results rigorously justify claiming without overreach.
Passing implementation tests demonstrates software correctness; it does not constitute empirical validation of unexecuted scientific hypotheses. Stage 5 and Stage 6 are closed, locked, and archived. Stage 6M execution remains strictly on hold pending formal academic supervisor authorization.

To solve this challenge, we developed the Disparity Safety Gate (DSG), a runtime supervisory controller. The DSG maintains a candidate model branch in memory, evaluates candidate updates against a frozen, speaker-disjoint, labeled Sentinel Panel (S), calculates paired speaker-cluster bootstrap Upper Confidence Bounds (UCB), and permits live deployment only if prospective subgroup regression (ΔG) and disparity amplification (ΔD) remain within pre-registered tolerances.

3. Comprehensive Literature Context & Research Gap Analysis

3.1 Prior Art in ASR & Test-Time Adaptation

Foundational self-supervised acoustic models such as Wav2Vec 2.0 (Baevski et al., 2020), HuBERT (Hsu et al., 2021), and Data2Vec (Baevski et al., 2022) have revolutionized speech recognition. However, extensive audits (Koenecke et al., 2020; Markl, 2022) document severe error rate disparities against non-standard dialects, non-native accents, and demographic subgroups.

Test-time adaptation paradigms including TENT (Wang et al., 2021), SUTA (Chen et al., 2022), CoTTA (Wang et al., 2022), and SAR (Niu et al., 2023) attempt to mitigate distribution shifts unsupervised by minimizing prediction entropy H(pθ) and maximizing output diversity D(pθ).

3.2 The Identified Research Gap

All existing CTTA frameworks suffer from three critical blind spots:

  • Unconditional Deployment: Every adaptation step is immediately deployed to live inference without verification.
  • Global Aggregation Fallacy: Models are benchmarked solely on macro-averaged WER, completely masking localized catastrophic regressions in minority accent groups.
  • Absence of Reversal Mechanisms: Once an unlabelled update collapses phoneme boundaries (e.g., merging vowels under noise), error compounding permanently degrades future predictions.

5. Chronological Project Evolution: What We Have Done in the Past

The research progressed through seven sequential, evidence-driven stages governed by strict empirical gating criteria:

Stage-by-Stage Technical Milestones

Stage 0 – Protocol Freeze (v1.0.0-canonical): Formal pre-registration of research questions, exact mathematical estimands (ΔR, ΔG, ΔD), and the Mandatory Scientific Label Invariant Rule.

Stage 1 – Data Engineering & Verification Suite: Implementation of audio ingestion (16 kHz mono conversion), WADA-SNR extraction, speech rate measurement (syllables/words per second), text normalization (exact casing, punctuation removal, number expansion), and 5-way speaker-disjoint splitting. Constructed a 90-test automated suite ensuring 0% speaker leakage, seed reproducibility, and label isolation.

Stage 2 – Static Baseline & GLMM Confounder Audit (L2-ARCTIC): Benchmarked all 6 models on 6 native accent groups (Arabic, Mandarin, Spanish, Hindi, Korean, Vietnamese). Discovered immense static disparity: D = 14.13% to 47.83%. Fit Poisson Generalized Linear Mixed Models (GLMM) with speaker random intercepts, proving that accent disparity remains statistically significant (Rate Ratio RR = 1.124×, p < 0.05) even after strictly controlling for acoustic confounders (SNR, speaking rate, duration).

Stage 3 – CTTA Discovery Pilot on Canonical Stream: Evaluated unsupervised SUTA adaptation on clean studio-recorded L2-ARCTIC speech. Major Discovery: Adaptation on clean speech was benign and stable (ΔR ≤ −0.37%, zero subgroup regression). Following our preregistered protocol, DSG was not prematurely deployed, maintaining scientific integrity.

Stage 4 & 4.1 – Empirical Boundary & Acoustic Stress Characterization: Tested 4 real-world acoustic perturbations (15dB Gaussian noise, 5dB severe noise, 15dB babble noise, T60 = 0.4s reverberation). Subgroup regressions up to +3.26% were empirically uncovered, calibrating practical significance thresholds (δG = 0.02, δD = 0.02) and officially authorizing DSG deployment.

Stage 5 Pre-Flight & Migration (Common Voice 27.0): Upstream deprecation of Common Voice 11.0 necessitated protocol amendment v1.0-cv27-amended. Curated N=900 evaluation clips (60 speakers across 6 strata: Australia, Canada, England, Ireland, South Asia, US) and M=300 sentinel panel clips (30 strictly disjoint speakers), bound by cryptographic SHA-256 locks.

Stage 5A–5C – First Run & Forensic Discovery: Initial streaming adaptation triggered a 100% fail-closed rejection rate. A rigorous root-cause audit revealed an audio resolver defect: the sentinel evaluator was searching an obsolete relative directory path, returning empty waveforms. The fail-closed contract functioned perfectly (refusing to accept corrupted audio updates), but the implementation required path repair.

Stage 5D–5E – Materialization & Primary Empirical Breakthrough: Materialized all audio files and executed full streaming adaptation. Ungated SUTA degraded overall WER by +1.10 percentage points (+95 errors). The DSG accepted 9 safe updates and rejected 216 unsafe updates, holding degradation to +0.07 percentage points—preventing 93.68% (89/95) of all adaptation-induced errors!

Stage 6 & 6.1 – Cross-Backbone Scaling Across Six CTC Models: Scaled validation to all six CTC architectures. Demonstrated that SUTA vulnerability is universal across self-supervised acoustic encoders, and proved DSG consistently prevents degradation across all backbones (23 updates accepted out of 1,350 evaluated windows, 0 runtime crashes).

6. What We Modified in the Plan: Pivots, Course Corrections, & Lessons Learned

Over the course of the research, multiple empirical observations and environmental constraints prompted deliberate methodological course corrections:

Dimension Initial / Planned Approach Modified Execution & Methodological Rationale
1. Evaluation Paradigm Static, batch-level cross-sectional evaluations of adaptation checkpoints. Shifted to Prequential Streaming: Testing strictly precedes adaptation (Score Yt with θt → Adapt) to accurately capture real-time acoustic time-series drift.
2. Pre-Registration Discipline Deploying DSG immediately upon building the controller code. Held DSG in Stage 3: Because clean audio produced zero regression, deploying DSG would have been an uninformative false victory. Stress testing (Stage 4) was introduced to find the true failure boundary.
3. External Dataset Migration Common Voice 11.0 archive as specified in the original protocol. Migrated to CV 27.0: Mozilla deprecated CV 11.0 mirrors. Formal amendment v1.0-cv27-amended established cryptographically locked manifests with zero speaker overlap.
4. Audio Resolver Engineering Implicit path resolution assuming relative dataset directories. Explicit Cryptographic Resolver: After Stage 5A forensic audit uncovered missing paths, implemented absolute path resolution with integrity pre-flight checks.
5. Adaptation Horizon Single-step transitions strictly resetting from the base model θ0. Multi-Step Trajectory Horizon (Stage 6M): Recognized that real-world deployment accumulates updates across steps (θ0 → θ1 ... → θt), requiring multi-step rollout metrics (h ∈ {1, 3, 5}).
6. Counterfactual Auditing Discarding rejected candidate weights in-memory during streaming. The Candidate Replay Vault: Mandated serializing all rejected candidate models θ't to disk, enabling downstream counterfactual simulation of what would have happened if deployed.
💡 Methodological Takeaway: The Value of Fail-Closed Engineering
The Stage 5A forensic discovery provided crucial empirical validation for fail-closed architectural design. When audio paths were broken, an unconstrained CTTA system would have silently ingested empty noise buffers and corrupted model representations. The DSG's fail-closed gate recognized the performance degradation and rejected 100% of the updates, protecting the system from catastrophic data corruption.

9. Threats to Validity, Honest Limitations, & Anti-Overclaim Mandate

9.1 Methodological Limitations

  • Acoustic Stress Approximations: Convolving room impulse responses (T60=0.4s) and injecting noise accurately reflects synthetic degradation, but does not fully reproduce non-stationary, conversational, overlapping speech.
  • Compute Envelope Constraints: Restricting execution to a single laptop (CPU & 16GB RAM) constrained streaming sequences to T=30 windows (N=900 clips) and model sizes to ≤ 317M parameters.
  • Finite Demographics: Six coarse regional categories represent broad geographic accents, but do not capture micro-dialects or sociolects.

9.2 The Anti-Overclaim Mandate

  • What DSG Proves: DSG rigorously proves that pre-deployment sentinel screening halts adaptation-induced collapse and bounds subgroup regression on distribution shifts aligned with the sentinel panel.
  • What DSG Does NOT Prove: DSG does not eliminate static baseline model bias; it does not guarantee positive adaptation gain when unlabelled audio is overwhelmingly noisy; and it does not guarantee safety on orthogonal, unrepresented accents outside the sentinel distribution.

10. Future Research Directions & Scientific Conclusion

High-Priority Roadmap Directions:

  1. Multilingual Dialect Portability: Extending DSG to low-resource Indian linguistic varieties (IndicTIMIT, Kathbath) and tonal languages.
  2. Active Dynamic Sentinel Panels: Transitioning from frozen sentinel panels to online dynamically curated sentinel buffers that actively synthesize difficult boundary cases.
  3. Hardware Acceleration & Latency Optimization: Applying INT8 quantization and ONNX Runtime to reduce bootstrap screening latency for sub-100ms real-time edge streaming.

Scientific Conclusion: This research establishes that Continual Test-Time Adaptation without safety constraints poses severe risks of disparity amplification and subgroup regression. By introducing the Disparity Safety Gate with paired speaker-cluster bootstrapping, we have demonstrated that autonomous adaptation updates can be statistically regulated in real time, preventing 93.68% of adaptation errors and establishing a foundational blueprint for equitable, safety-critical speech recognition.
